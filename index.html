04 / TESTING THE DECISIONTHE TWO-CHOICE PROTOCOL

Read the evidence.
Check the judgment.

01

The past is visible.
The correction is not.

Each case starts at a frozen research state. Later interventions and work are used to construct the case, not included in the model’s project snapshot.

02

Same question.
Both option orders.

Each question is asked with the reference plan first and with it second. Correct selections are counted across both orders to reduce position bias.

03

With the project.
And without it.

An options-only arm tests how much can be inferred from wording alone. Comparing the two arms helps examine case quality and the use of project evidence.

SOURCE AND SCOPE

Agon supplies the research histories. The benchmark evaluates models making Scientist decisions within those histories. Its cases reflect this research workflow; the reference labels are not a claim that every other scientific approach is invalid.
